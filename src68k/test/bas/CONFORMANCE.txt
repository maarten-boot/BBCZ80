The c_*.in tests are the BBC BASIC V conformance suite of the Agon port
(tests/conformance/l*.bas in https://github.com/AgonPlatform/agon-bbc-basic,
by Jeroen Venema, at commit 2998edd of https://github.com/maarten-boot/agon-bbc-basic),
under the zlib licence below.

Altered for this port: line 50 "MODE 0" (an Agon screen mode) is a REM, and
RUN is added at the end. The programs check themselves and print PASSED and
FAILED counts; the .exp files are the 68k output with "FAILED : 0" (the CP/M
Z80 cannot run them all: arrays over 64K, CP/M's 128-byte file records).
